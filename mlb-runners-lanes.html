Methodology, definitions & data coverage

Runner state: Eight distinct configurations: empty, 1B, 2B, 1B+2B, 3B, 1B+3B, 2B+3B, and loaded. Runner occupancy is reconstructed from recorded MLB runner movements, and attributed at the start of the plate appearance, even when a runner steals or advances later in that plate appearance. RISP situations are the lanes containing a runner on second or third.
Measures: AVG = H / AB. OBP = (H+BB+HBP) / (AB+BB+HBP+SF). K%, BB%, and HR% use completed plate appearances. In the pitcher view, these are opponent rates; higher K% and lower AVG/OBP/BB%/HR% are better. "Best / Worst" respects those directions. Each lane ranks players independently. Minimum AB applies to AVG, minimum PA to the other metrics. Coverage: MLB StatsAPI play-by-play is retrieved game-by-game; loaded games are shown immediately, and final-game data are saved in IndexedDB. Missing or unrecognized events are flagged rather than estimated. Full-season loads may be slow on the first visit.
