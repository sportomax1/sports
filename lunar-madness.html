Research safeguards

High scoring is defined using each league's observed 90th-percentile combined score. Close games use league-specific final margins. A final score cannot establish an upset or a comeback: those require pregame probabilities or play-by-play/period scoring, respectively.

Compare event rates per completed game, not raw event totals. Season, league, schedule density and playoff status can confound any apparent moon effect.
