High scoring ⓘCombined final score in the top 10% for its league. Threshold is calculated from loaded games.
Comebacks ⓘA winner previously trailed substantially. Requires verified play-by-play or period scores; not inferred from final results.
Upsets ⓘA documented pregame underdog wins. Historical pregame odds are required; not inferred from team names or final scores.

Game details

High scoring: top 10% of combined scores within each league's loaded sample (minimum 10 games). Upsets use documented pregame favorites. Comebacks require a winner to overcome a period-end deficit of 10+ points (football/basketball), 3+ runs (baseball), or 2+ goals (hockey). Unverifiable games are excluded. Moon illumination is approximate.
