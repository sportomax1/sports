Data & limitations

The NFL, NBA, and NHL feeds are undocumented ESPN endpoints. Their filters, history and coverage can change without notice. The MLB feed is MLB StatsAPI and supports exact date-range requests.

  • For ESPN, the requested date range is filtered against returned records. If the API does not supply the entire period, this app cannot guarantee that zero results means no transactions occurred.
  • Player portraits are matched to Sports Hub's cached ESPN player indexes. Headshots may be absent for former players, free agents, or ambiguous names.
  • Results are temporarily cached in your browser. Refresh bypasses the transaction cache.

For source verification, consult the official NFL transactions and each league's published transactions.
