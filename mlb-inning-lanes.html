How it works: Each column represents the game inning (not a player's first or second inning of an appearance). Batter AVG = hits ÷ official at-bats in that inning. Pitcher OPP AVG = hits allowed ÷ opponents' official at-bats while that pitcher was on the mound in that game inning. Lowest is best for pitchers; highest is best for batters. Extra innings (10+) are grouped. Walks, hit-by-pitch, sacrifices and interference do not count as at-bats. Day/week/month/season calculations use individual MLB play-by-play records, not a pre-aggregated inning stat. A full season processes thousands of games; previous final games are cached locally in IndexedDB. Incomplete/live games can change. Data: MLB StatsAPI.
