Methodology & questions

What decides the CFP field in 2026?

The 12-team field includes the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12 and SEC champions, the highest-ranked team from the American, CUSA, MAC, Mountain West, Pac-12 or Sun Belt, and seven at-large selections. Notre Dame qualifies automatically if in the committee's top 12, reducing at-large availability. Seeds 1–4 get byes. The app estimates rankings; it does not claim to reproduce committee decisions.

What about the 2025 season?

The 2025 CFP used 12 teams with automatic qualification for the five highest-ranked conference champions, regardless of conference. The four highest-ranked teams earned byes. Notre Dame's special top-12 automatic qualification starts in 2026. This app models those historical 2025 selection rules separately.

What changes in 2027 and later?

The official 24-team format applies from the 2027 season through 2031. Four A4 champions plus the best G6 team qualify automatically, alongside 19 other highest-ranked teams. The top eight get byes, seeds 9–16 host 17–24, and seeds 1–8 host round-two games. Conference championship game formats remain under each conference's control.

How are bowl matchups predicted?

After excluding projected playoff teams, the model estimates six-win eligibility from completed games and the remaining schedule, then greedily matches candidates to bowls by illustrative affiliations, availability and ratings. FCS win limits, academic-rate exceptions, special bowl selection agreements, declined invitations and changing contracts aren't fully enforceable with public scoreboard data.

How are conference standings computed?

Confirmed conference games are counted only when both ESPN team conference IDs match. Teams are ranked by conference win percentage, then conference wins and model rating. This is NOT an official conference standings tie-break, and conference champions are projections until decided.

How do CFP, AP and Coaches rankings affect the projection?

Auto chooses the latest eligible CFP poll once published, otherwise the AP/Coaches consensus, then the independent model. You can select a single poll, combine polls, or use results-only mode. The influence slider sets the published-poll weight versus the game-results model. A selected but unavailable poll automatically falls back to the results model and is explicitly flagged. The official CFP committee does not use this formula; its final rankings determine the actual bracket.

What happens to unranked teams?

Published Top 25 rankings are incomplete by design. Unranked teams retain their earned results-model score, with an estimated below-Top-25 poll floor. The projection never presents inferred ranks as official positions.

How does the predictive score work?

The model blends wins and losses, opponent performance, adjusted scoring margin, home/away outcomes, and quality wins. Preseason estimates use prior-year records and rankings when available; the previous season's influence shrinks as current games accumulate. ESPN polls influence rankings only if the response date is not later than your selected cutoff. This is not official CFP math.

Does choosing an old date leak future scores?

No. Only completed games with a kickoff date at or before the selected cutoff are counted. Scheduled opponents later in the season may be considered for projected bowl eligibility, but their later results are excluded. Polls returned with dates after the cutoff are rejected.

Why are some conferences or bowls missing?

ESPN's public, undocumented feeds are not guaranteed. The app rejects unknown or ambiguous NCAA fallback team matches and discloses missing weeks. It never fills missing results with invented games. A projected bracket is withheld when there aren't enough verified FBS teams.

Can I use this as a single HTML file?

Yes. All CSS and JavaScript is embedded. Data and logos are requested over the internet. Open this file directly or host it on GitHub Pages. Live data access depends on browser CORS policies and third-party API availability; no server or API key is required.
