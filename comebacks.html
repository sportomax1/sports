How it works: A comeback is a completed win by a team that trailed earlier. Play-by-play records supply the largest observed in-game deficit; when those records are unavailable, complete inning/quarter/period/half-end scores provide a conservative lower bound, clearly labeled on each card. Games without a verifiable score timeline are excluded rather than guessed. Hockey shootout outcomes and incomplete ESPN feeds may be omitted. Totals describe the games successfully inspected, not an official league-wide record. Season scans can require many ESPN requests; use Stop to cancel.
