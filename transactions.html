Sources & coverage

Transaction Wire combines ESPN's undocumented NFL, NBA and NHL endpoints with MLB StatsAPI's date-range transaction feed.

  • ESPN may return recent transactions only. Filtering by a historical date does not guarantee full coverage.
  • Player photos and logos are matched against existing Sports Hub player indexes and provider images. Some former, traded, or free-agent players may not have a portrait.
  • Results are cached in IndexedDB for responsiveness. Force Refresh re-requests the feeds.
