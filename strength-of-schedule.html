Methodology: Rankings use only regular-season games. For a selected team, each game contributes its opponent's winning percentage, including repeat divisional matchups. Average opponent W% (default) = arithmetic mean of the winning percentages for each game's opponent, counting repeat matchups. Combined opponent W% = sum of opponents' wins plus half their ties, divided by their total decisions (opponent records are counted once per scheduled matchup). Current-season records are reconstructed from completed games as of the chosen week/date or latest available results; previous-season records use final completed regular-season results. NHL overtime/shootout losses count as non-wins. College rankings include teams with sufficiently complete Division I/FBS schedules; lower-division opponents may lack representative records. No preseason or postseason games are included. Unrated opponents are excluded, with coverage disclosed. Data: ESPN scoreboards (unofficial, undocumented endpoint; availability can change).
