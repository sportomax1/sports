Six statistical specialties

PTS is always in the starting five. The bench owns STL. No player can occupy more than one role, even when they lead multiple categories.

How selection works

  • Flexible roles: no artificial PG/SG/SF/PF/C restrictions.
  • Normalize each category against its eligible leader, then maximize the combined six-role score with unique-player constraints.
  • Daily: counts from one date. Monthly and season: per-game rates by default, with an adjustable minimum games threshold.
  • Only completed ESPN game box scores count. Season scans can be lengthy on first use; results are cached locally.
