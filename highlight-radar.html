How ranking works: completed games are graded on finish closeness, scoring, recorded lead changes, comebacks, late tension and overtime. Upcoming or live games show a separate matchup-potential estimate based on available team records and rankings. Analysis quality: “Play by play” indicates detailed event data; “Period scores” gives only a lower-bound view of lead changes; “Basic” uses available final scores. Results, winner, score breakdown, and game specifics stay concealed until an individual game is revealed. Revealing one game leaves every other game hidden; the top control can reveal or hide all games. YouTube caution: clicking “Find highlights” opens YouTube search, whose titles, thumbnails or recommendations may disclose the result. Data: ESPN's unofficial public feed, subject to availability; estimates are editorial, not verified TV ratings.
